About Us

Welcome to KristAl Wellness Studio, where your beauty is our care. We are dedicated to providing a sanctuary of beauty & relaxation tailored to your needs. Our philosophy blends effective body therapies with delicate beauty rituals to help you unwind, revitalize, and glow.

We specialize in a holistic approach to wellbeing. Whether you need to reduce muscle tension, improve circulation, or simply escape the daily stress, our Wellness Massages are designed to restore your balance. We utilize nature's elements—bamboo, hot stones, and warm salt—to offer deep relaxation and detoxification.

Experience the difference with our Sugaring Epilation. A delicate, natural, and less painful alternative that leaves your skin smooth, velvety, and bright. Ideal for even the most sensitive skin, our method ensures long-lasting results and fewer ingrown hairs. Pamper yourself and feel the difference from the very first visit!
